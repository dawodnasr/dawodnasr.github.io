MORPH DRESS

  • 100% Natural Silk
  • Lost-wool construction
  • Mirror polish
  • Finished finish
Request info

[STORY]

This piece was born almost playfully, starting from the form of a realistic beetle that was deliberately deconstructed and transformed into something new. The rigor of the figure was released, giving way to freedom and experimentation, dissolving into a more abstract and spontaneous gesture. A light yet meaningful creative process that speaks of transformation: as in life, what ends does not disappear, but changes form and continues to exist, revealing unexpected beauty.

[CRAFTING]

Handcrafted using the lost-wool technique. Born from an initial model later transformed, it retains traces of its origin in the open, fragmented structure, evoking at once an eye or a void. The irregular surface, etched with lines that seem to melt away, recalls volcanic matter or a process of decomposition—turning what fades into a new kind of beauty.

[STORY]

The design draws inspiration from the search for both inner and everyday balance. Two opposing ends — one flat, the other inclined — engage in a dialogue between stability and tension. A balance never final, always on the verge of shifting, embodying the constant oscillation that defines life itself.

ROCK DRESS

  • 100% Natural Silk
  • Lost wool construction
  • Mirror polish
  • Oxidized finish
Request info

[STORY]

The 'Rock' piece was born from the desire to transform the strength of stone, sculpted by time, into a garment. Its marked surface speaks of erosion by the elements: one side strong and resistant, the other crossed by fissures that let the light seep through. An imperfect balance that reflects the beauty of nature and the memory it carries.

[CRAFTING]

Piece handcrafted using the couture atelier technique. The process creates a textured surface with cavities and reliefs that enhance its sculptural character. The irregular shape and the play of light and shadow make each piece different from the other, celebrating the artisanal process that makes it truly unique.

UNDERTOW DRESS

  • 100% Natural Silk
  • Lost-wool construction
  • Oxidized surface with finished details
  • Relaxed, adjustable silhouette
Request info

[STORY]

This piece was born from the desire to capture the slow movement of seaweed beneath the surface — their silent, vertical sway in a world where time seems suspended. It embodies the deep calm and slowness we often lose today, that fleeting breath we can only sense underwater, in a silence that restores and envelops.

[CRAFTING]

Piece handcrafted using the couture atelier technique. The relaxed, adjustable cut has been oxidized to emphasize its depth and detail. The design is inspired by seaweed, climbing the finger as if defying gravity, with vertical and irregular forms that evoke the fluid, organic movement of the ocean floor.
